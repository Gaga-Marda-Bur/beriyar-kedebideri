2. Categories of Data Collected

  • Guest use (no account): You can use the app and access lessons and exercises without creating an account; interface language, local progress, and cached audio remain on your device only. When sending guest technical feedback, the server does not store a persistent device_id.
  • Optional account data (adults): If you register or sign in, we process your username, optional email, and password (stored in hashed form).
  • Session and authenticated learning progress: User ID, unit/lesson progress, review state, sync credentials, and authentication session tokens (sessionid / csrf).
  • Optional technical feedback: Report title and message text, plus any screenshot or audio file you voluntarily attach (attachments are stored in a separate private R2 bucket).
  • Data not collected: The app does not collect date of birth, does not request camera, microphone, location, or contacts permissions, and includes no advertising, payments, or third-party ad tracking SDKs.

2. Catégories de données collectées

  • Utilisation en mode invité (sans compte) : Vous pouvez utiliser l’application et suivre les leçons et exercices sans créer de compte ; la langue d’interface, la progression locale et l’audio mis en cache restent uniquement sur votre appareil. 4. Verified Infrastructure and International Transfers

The platform relies on the following technical infrastructure as verified in provider dashboards:

  • Render Web (Free plan — configured region: Oregon, United States): Application and API server; the visible log consultation window in the Render dashboard is 7 days.
  • Render PostgreSQL (Starter plan — configured region: Oregon, United States): Relational database; Point-in-Time Recovery (PITR) covers 3 days. A PostgreSQL export dated 15 November 2026 also exists with a local copy.

  • Cloudflare Logpush: Subscription is not enabled according to the dashboard.

Distinction between visible windows and internal erasure: The durations stated above (7-day Render log window, 30-day Cloudflare Email Routing history, 3-day PostgreSQL PITR) reflect the consultation or recovery windows visible to the operator; they do not demonstrate the exact underlying internal erasure timelines of each third-party provider, which are not all independently demonstrated.

Using the service may involve international data transfers outside the user’s country of residence (Render in the United States, Cloudflare R2 in Eastern Europe). This external hosting choice reflects the operator’s lack of self-hosted infrastructure; no specific legal guarantee regarding international transfers is demonstrated or promised, as the service relies solely on the standard terms of the selected providers.

5. Data Retention Schedule (Conditional Targets Requiring Manual Execution)

BK retains only the data needed for learning, security, and service operation. The following retention schedule has been conditionally approved as operational targets:

  • Accounts and authenticated progress: Lifetime of the account.
  • Inactive accounts: Review considered after 24 months, with no automatic purge currently active.
  • Recent guest progress: Stored locally on your device only until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.
  • Closed technical feedback: Target retention of 12 months.
  • Feedback media stored in R2: Target deletion no later than 90 days after report closure, via manual controlled deletion.
  • Django sessions: 14-day expiration (or immediate termination on sign-out or account deletion), with periodic cleanup of expired sessions to be organized.
  • Account deletion requests: Target processing within 30 calendar days, subject to applicable legal obligations.
  • Backups and technical logs: Subject to the real limits stated in Section 4 (3-day PITR, local export of 15 November 2026, visible Render and Cloudflare log windows) and applicable operating procedures.

Important operational note: No automatic server-side or R2 purge job is currently enabled; manual purge procedures must therefore be executed by the operator to achieve these targets.

7. Your Rights, Account Deletion, and Complementary Feedback Operations

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by writing to privacy@beriyarkedebideri.com, or submit a password-verified deletion request from the Android app settings or via /account-deletion/.

  • Manual administrative processing (not instantaneous): Submitting a request creates a reference code (DEL-...) without immediate automatic erasure; an administrator verifies and executes deletion manually within the 30-calendar-day target.
  • Scope of atomic account erasure: Atomic administrative execution deletes the user account, associated learning progress, sync credentials, and active sessions in a single transaction; detaches and anonymizes technical feedback links (clearing user and device_id); dissociates the contributor link from public No Ena publications; and scrubs the username and free-text note from the deletion request record while keeping a minimal non-identifying audit trace (reference code, status, timestamps).
  • Complementary feedback redaction and R2 media deletion: Atomic account erasure does not automatically scrub free-text feedback content or delete attached media files in private R2. Where applicable (such as personal data inside report text or attachments, or upon retention expiry), the administrator performs two separate confirmed manual actions: (1) manual PII redaction of feedback titles/messages, and (2) controlled R2 media deletion from the private bucket with database metadata cleanup.
  • Backup and log limits: Instantaneous and complete erasure from all existing backups is not promised; historical snapshots remain in PostgreSQL Point-in-Time Recovery (3 days), in the local PostgreSQL export of 15 November 2026, and in external provider logs until their rotation or retention cycles expire.

7. Vos droits, suppression de compte et opérations complémentaires

Vous pouvez demander l’accès, la rectification ou la suppression de vos données en écrivant à privacy@beriyarkedebideri.com, ou déposer une demande confirmée par mot de passe depuis les réglages de l’application Android ou la page /account-deletion/.

  • Traitement administratif manuel (non instantané) : L’envoi d’une demande enregistre un code de référence (DEL-...) sans suppression automatique immédiate ; l’administrateur vérifie et exécute l’effacement manuellement, avec un objectif de 30 jours calendaires.
  • Périmètre de la suppression atomique du compte : L’exécution administrative atomique supprime le compte utilisateur, les progressions pédagogiques associées, les jetons de synchronisation et les sessions actives ; détache et anonymise les signalements techniques (suppression du lien user et du device_id) ; dissocie le lien contributeur des publications publiques No Ena ; et purge l’identifiant ainsi que la note libre de la demande de suppression en ne conservant qu’une trace minimale non identifiante (référence, statut, dates).
  • Opérations complémentaires de caviardage et de suppression R2 : La suppression atomique du compte ne caviarde pas automatiquement le texte libre des signalements et ne supprime pas automatiquement leurs pièces jointes sur R2. Lorsque nécessaire (présence de données personnelles ou échéance de conservation), l’administrateur exécute deux opérations manuelles distinctes avec confirmation explicite : (1) le caviardage manuel des données personnelles dans les titres et messages de feedback, et (2) la suppression contrôlée des médias de feedback dans le bucket privé R2 avec nettoyage des références en base.
  • Limites relatives aux sauvegardes et journaux : Une suppression instantanée et complète dans l’ensemble des sauvegardes n’est pas promise ; les instantanés existants subsistent dans le Point-in-Time Recovery PostgreSQL (3 jours), dans l’export PostgreSQL local du 15 novembre 2026 ainsi que dans les journaux des prestataires externes jusqu’à l’expiration de leur cycle de rotation ou de conservation.
